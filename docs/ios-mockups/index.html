iOS companion: screen mock-ups

For review. A read-only iPhone companion to the Mac ledger (PRD-ios.md). Every figure below is invented, and every one is computed on the Mac and carried in a snapshot. The phone computes nothing (PRD §10, shared-contract §2).

The labels follow the backend's own: estimated, reserved, planned, actual, budget pace, and the provisional reasons of D-072. A missing value says so, and is never shown as 0.
